## SUMMARY

Our intellectual property team, with its wide experience gained at international law firms, public sector policy and industry roles, offers clients a full range of intellectual property services. You can rely on our knowledge to safeguard your innovative businesses.

We represent a diverse mix of public and private sector clients on intellectual property protection matters, including anti-counterfeiting, trademarks, patents, designs, and copyrights, within Nigeria and across the African continent.

---

## ABOUT THE FIRM

NECS Legal offers a full spectrum of intellectual property services, drawing on a broad network to protect clients in Nigeria and Africa.
Our services include creating and clearing trademarks and designs, registering them, and managing portfolios with watching services and renewals. We document intellectual property transfers, provide strategic advice, handle domain name registration and disputes, and manage advertising and broadcasting standards complaints. We also provide patent advisory, dispute resolution, copyright support, and anti-counterfeiting measures regarding registered and unregistered intellectual property.
